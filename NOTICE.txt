The vehicle kinematics module (src/ufast/cosim/kinematics.py) reimplements
the edge travel-time model published with LogiFabSim (S. Rank, V. Betker,
IFAC-PapersOnLine, 2025). The SMT2020 datasets (dataset/HVLM, LVHM, LVLM)
originate from the SMT2020 semiconductor manufacturing testbed (D. Kopp,
M. Hassoun, A. Kalir, L. Moench, IEEE Transactions on Semiconductor
Manufacturing, 2020).
